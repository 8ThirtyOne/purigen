With special guest speaker Dr. Jian Gu of MD Anderson Cancer Center

 

FFPE tissue samples with a low abundance of nucleic acid continue to present a challenge for cancer genomics. Such samples frequently fail to produce sufficient DNA or RNA for downstream analyses, which can impact patient care or reduce the statistical power of a study. The automated Ionic® Purification System uses Isotachophoresis to increase quantity and quality of nucleic acids extracted from FFPE and requires 75% less hands-on time than column- or bead-based methods.

Join us for an on-demand seminar to learn more about the Ionic system and a real-world case study on how it's being utilized at a leading cancer center.
