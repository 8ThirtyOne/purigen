Dear BLI Sales Team,

We are pleased to announce the release of the first of our product demos, the Beacon General Product Demo. As you may have read from the communication sent out on May 10th, these will be short, 1-1.5 hours bead-based demos that will be delivered at each of our Innovation Centers. If you have any prospective customers who would benefit from this live, hands-on experience, please feel free to shared the promotion.  

To request a demo, please make certain to follow the Demo Request Process and contact the regional FAS manager and Innovation Center owner (both included in the email distribution lists listed above) as soon as possible but at least one month ahead. The FAS manager and Innovation Center owner will work on scheduling the demo based on FAS/TSS and Beacon availability. Please also make sure that you provide the information requested in the Demo Request Form.
