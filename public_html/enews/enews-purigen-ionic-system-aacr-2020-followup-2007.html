Thank You for Visiting Us at the AACR Virtual Meeting

Thank you for visiting Purigen Biosystems at the AACR 2020 Virtual Meeting. We are excited to help researchers and clinicians use our technology to recover more high-quality nucleic acid from their samples with more consistency and less effort than ever before.

Using isotachophoresis, our cutting-edge Ionic® Purification System separates nucleic acids freely in solution, without binding or stripping from physical surfaces. This produces a higher yield of pure nucleic acid in its native form for better results in downstream applications including qPCR and NGS.

 

Taking as little as 3 minutes of hand-on time to purify nucleic acid from FFPE tissue, purification using the Ionic system is faster and more reliable.
