Purigen Ionic® Purification System Now Available in Europe

Dear Researcher,

Purigen Biosystems is now offering the revolutionary Ionic® Purification System for automated purification of DNA and RNA from biological samples to researchers in Europe. This system uses a technique called isotachophoresis to provide an automated alternative to bead- and column-based nucleic acid purification methods with several advantages for downstream genomics application including NGS. For example, since our technology does not require any binding, washing, or stripping from fixed surfaces, it yields nucleic acids that are more intact and representative of the native DNA and RNA in the sample. Our recent data shows that this results in extracts that have no bias towards fragment length or GC content. Furthermore, without the typical bind, wash, and elute steps, the workflow is simple, with an average of just 5 minutes of hands-on time per sample.

When it comes to challenging samples like FFPE tissue, the Ionic system can produce up to 3x more DNA and 2x more RNA (including miRNA). The increased yield enables downstream analysis when traditional extraction methods would fail to generate sufficient material (as described in this Nature Research article). Similarly, the Ionic system can extract and purify nucleic acids from as few as 10 cells to enable molecular analyses from small subsets of patient cells over the course of therapy. These are just a few of the examples of what the Ionic system offers.

Given your work and its relevance to the cancer research space, I thought that you may be interested in learning more about our novel technology. I’m available to answer any questions or to provide more information.
