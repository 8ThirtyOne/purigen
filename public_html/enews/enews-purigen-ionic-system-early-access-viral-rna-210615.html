Ionic® Pure Viral RNA Kit – Early Access Program

Clinical laboratories need to rapidly develop new assays to detect aggressive, drug-resistant viruses, and obtaining sufficient high-quality RNA to reach target thresholds for detection is often a challenge. The Ionic Pure Viral RNA Kit is designed to overcome this challenge by providing high yields of pure RNA with a simple, automated, charge-based workflow. The same workflow was used by scientists from Purigen and the Stanford Microfluidics Laboratory who were selected as finalists for the XPRIZE Rapid Covid Testing Competition.

A Superior Approach to Nucleic Acid Purification

 
 
MORE INFO

The Ionic® Purification System is the next-gen sample prep solution that utilizes the principles of isotachophoresis to separate and extract pure DNA and RNA without the need to bind, wash, and strip nucleic acids from fixed surfaces. The workflow is simple, with minimal hands-on time, and it provides higher yields of pure nucleic acid. Scientists at some of the top cancer research facilities in the U.S. are realizing the significant advantages of the Ionic system and are using it to obtain valuable data from their most limited and challenging FFPE samples.
