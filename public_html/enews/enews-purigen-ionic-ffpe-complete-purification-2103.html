A Complete Solution for Purifying FFPE Samples

with 75% less hands-on time than conventional methods

The new Ionic® FFPE Complete Purification Kit enables simultaneous purification of DNA and RNA, including microRNA, from the same FFPE tissue section or scroll with a highly optimized workflow and minimal hands-on time. By simultaneously extracting both DNA and RNA, the Ionic FFPE Complete Purification kit delivers high yields while requiring 75% less hands-on time than conventional bead- and column-based extraction methods. The kit is designed for use with the revolutionary Ionic® Purification System, which utilizes isotachophoresis technology to extract DNA and RNA in their native form, with no binding or stripping from fixed surfaces.

  • Simple Workflow – Extract and purify DNA and RNA from FFPE scrolls or sections in one streamlined workflow
  • Purify DNA, mRNA and miRNA – RNA purification includes both mRNA, miRNA and other small non-coding RNAs
  • Simplify Lysis – De-paraffinize, lyse, and de-crosslink in a single reaction without using harsh chemicals
  • Improve Reliability – Minimize user-to-user variability, cross-contamination, and sample loss
